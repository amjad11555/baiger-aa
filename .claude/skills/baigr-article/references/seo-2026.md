# SEO + AEO ranking factors — 2026 (evidence base)

Verified against current sources (see bottom). Read once per session; this is the
"why" behind the workflow in SKILL.md. Ranking in 2026 is **system-based, not a
checklist** — match intent first, then build genuine depth and credibility.

## 1. The mental model has changed
- **Helpful Content is now core, not a periodic update.** People-first quality is
  continuously integrated into the ranking algorithm. Content written primarily to
  game search engines is systematically suppressed.
- **Google evaluates your whole footprint, not one URL.** A technically perfect page
  from an unknown author with no outside validation can lose to a simpler page from
  an entity Google already trusts. Authority compounds across the domain — which is
  exactly why BAIGR's blog sits in `baigr.com/blog` (subfolder consolidates authority
  into the main domain), and why internal linking to the site matters.

## 2. E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
Not a single score — a framework Google's systems approximate through many signals.
Operationalize it in the writing:
- **Experience**: first-hand, "here's how we actually run this for Gulf clients."
- **Expertise**: correct, specific, current detail; no vague generalities.
- **Authoritativeness**: cite and link real sources; be the comprehensive answer.
- **Trustworthiness** (the most important leg): accurate, honest, no fabrication,
  clear sourcing, no over-claiming. One fake statistic can sink the whole piece.

## 3. Search intent comes first
Before depth, before keywords: nail what the searcher actually wants
(informational / commercial-investigation / transactional / navigational). The top
results already reveal the intent Google rewards for that query — match it, then
exceed it.

## 4. Structure for humans AND for extraction
- **One H1** with the primary keyword, reading like a real title.
- **H2s** for major sections, each strong enough to stand alone (benefit- or
  question-driven). **H3s** for steps, examples, edge cases, FAQ items.
- **A 2–4 sentence direct-answer block near the top** is the single most citable
  element for featured snippets and AI Overviews. Put one under the H1, and lead
  key sections with a crisp extractable answer before elaborating.
- Scannability (short paragraphs, lists, bolded terms) helps readers and gives
  crawlers/AI clean extraction points.

## 5. Word count / depth
No magic number. Benchmark the top 3–5 ranking pages for the query as your **floor**,
then cover everything they cover and fill the gaps they missed. For BAIGR pillar
posts that means ~3000–5000 words when the query genuinely warrants it — never pad
to hit a count; padding trips the helpful-content signals.

## 6. AEO / GEO — getting cited by AI answer engines
AI Overviews now appear on a large and growing share of Google searches (roughly a
quarter to a half depending on query type), and users increasingly ask ChatGPT/
Perplexity/Gemini directly. To be the source they quote:
- **Extractable structure**: state definitions, comparisons, steps, and clear
  positions in self-contained sentences that lift cleanly into a summary.
- **Schema / JSON-LD**: tell engines exactly what each block is (Article, FAQ, HowTo).
- **Freshness**: AI citations skew hard to recently-updated pages (the strong
  majority updated within ~12 months, many within 6). Use current data; date the
  post; refresh evergreen posts.
- **Semantic completeness**: build a web of related concepts, synonyms, and links to
  high-authority entities so the piece reads as a topically complete answer.
- **Authority/E-E-A-T** again: trusted sources get cited more.

## 7. On-page specifics
- **Title tag** ≤ ~60 chars, primary keyword early; include the year when it's
  honestly relevant (signals freshness).
- **Meta description** ~120–160 chars, includes the keyword and a soft call to
  action; mobile truncates earlier, so front-load meaning.
- **Slug**: short, hyphenated, keyworded.
- **Internal links** with descriptive anchors; **external links** to authoritative
  sources build trust.

## 8. Image / infographic SEO
- **Alt text**: descriptive, keyword-inclusive, ~5–15 words, ideally < 125 chars
  (screen readers truncate). One image per content point beats a wall of text.
- **Descriptive filenames** (e.g. `meta-ads-funnel-gulf.webp`, not `img_01.png`).
- **Infographics** earn links and dwell time and are strong AI/visual-search fodder;
  give each stage a descriptive filename + detailed alt.
- **WebP** is the best all-round format for weight (25–35% smaller than JPEG at
  equal quality) — matters for Core Web Vitals.

## 9. Arabic / RTL specifics
- Arabic SEO is **not translation**: search behavior, phrasing, dialect, and cultural
  context differ. Target the terms Gulf users actually type, not only formal Arabic.
- Technical: `dir="rtl"`, proper Arabic font rendering, RTL-correct layout.
- Performance watch-out: Arabic RTL pages often lag on **INP**, and Arabic font
  files are heavy (300–500KB) — keep the page light so speed doesn't drag ranking.
