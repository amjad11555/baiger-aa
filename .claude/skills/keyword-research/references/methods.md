# Keyword methods without paid tools

Practical ways to research keywords using free/available sources. Useful because
paid tools underreport Arabic and a new site needs winnable targets, not vanity volume.

## 1. Mining Google Search Console (best source once live)
Once the site has impressions, Search Console → **Performance** → **Queries** is gold:
- **Positions 5–20** = you already rank but not top; a stronger, better-targeted page
  can lift these fast. These are the highest-ROI targets.
- **High impressions, low CTR** = you show up but the title/meta don't sell the click
  → a `hook-copywriting` pass, not new content.
- **Queries you didn't target** = real demand you can build a page around.
Filter by country (Saudi/UAE/etc.) to see the Gulf reality specifically.

## 2. Competitor-gap method
1. Identify the top 3–5 pages ranking for your seed query (WebSearch the seed).
2. Read their titles, H2/H3s, and FAQ — that's their keyword map.
3. List sub-topics/questions they cover. Your article must cover the union of them
   **plus** the gaps they missed (an angle, a local nuance, a newer data point).
4. Note terms that appear across multiple competitors — those are core to the topic
   and should appear naturally in your piece for topical completeness.

## 3. Autocomplete & PAA harvesting
- Type the seed in Arabic into Google; record every autocomplete suggestion (these
  are real, frequent queries).
- Add modifiers to surface long-tail: كيف، أفضل، سعر (avoid — no prices), طريقة،
  الأفضل، لِ + audience، + city/country.
- Expand "People Also Ask" boxes and record the questions → these become H2s/FAQ and
  often win featured snippets.

## 4. Estimating difficulty without a tool
Judge the competition of the pages currently ranking on page 1:
- Big national brands / marketplaces / gov sites ⇒ **hard**; skip for now.
- Forums, thin blogs, off-topic or weak pages ranking ⇒ **soft** target, go for it.
- Very few exact-match results ⇒ low competition, often a quick win.
- More words in the query (long-tail) ⇒ usually easier and higher-intent.

## 5. Prioritization heuristics for a newer domain
- Start with **long-tail + question + local** keywords (winnable now, high intent).
- Build **clusters** so early wins compound into topical authority.
- Queue **head terms** as pillar pages you'll strengthen as authority grows.
- Revisit Search Console monthly and promote "position 5–20" queries into their own
  optimized pieces.

## 6. Turning research into the plan
For each cluster: one pillar (head term) + 4–8 supporting posts (long-tail/questions),
all interlinking to the pillar and to `baigr.com/` service pages. Sequence: quick wins
first (momentum + early traffic), then fill the pillar and its cluster. Feed the chosen
keyword + secondaries straight into the `baigr-article` skill.
