# Hook & Headline Copywriting

The headline is 80% of the job: most people decide to read or scroll from the title
alone. Your task is to make the title irresistible **and honest** — because a
click won bait-and-switch is lost immediately (high bounce hurts SEO and burns
trust, which is fatal for a brand like BAIGR that sells credibility). Magnetic, not
misleading.

## The one rule that governs everything
**Promise something specific and valuable, then make sure the content delivers it.**
Curiosity without payoff is clickbait. Curiosity + a real payoff is great marketing.

## How to work
1. **Know the reader and the one promise.** Who is this for, what do they want or
   fear, and what is the single most compelling true benefit of the piece? Everything
   flows from that one promise.
2. **Generate widely, then cut.** Produce **10–15 headline options** across different
   angles (below). Quantity first — the best headline is usually the 12th, not the
   1st. Then score and pick.
3. **Pick a winner + 2 alternatives**, and explain in one line why the winner wins.
4. **Write the matching hook** (first 2–3 sentences) and the **meta description**.

## Angles to generate across (mix them)
Don't write 12 variations of the same idea — pull different levers:
- **Ultra-specific number / result**: "٧ أخطاء تقتل إعلانات متجرك على ميتا (وأنت لا تدري)"
- **Curiosity gap**: hint at a surprising insight without revealing it.
- **How-to / promise of a skill**: "كيف تكتب إعلاناً يبيع وأنت نائم".
- **Question that hits a pain**: "ليش إعلاناتك تجيب نقرات… بلا مبيعات؟".
- **Contrarian / myth-break**: "انسَ الميزانية الكبيرة — هذا ما يهمّ فعلاً".
- **Fear / cost of inaction**: what they lose by ignoring this.
- **Desire / transformation**: the after-state they want.
- **Speed / ease**: "في ٣٠ دقيقة"، "بدون خبرة تقنية".
- **Social proof / authority** (only if true): real, not invented.

## The 4 U's — score every finalist
A strong headline hits most of these; the winner should hit at least three:
- **Useful** — clear benefit to the reader.
- **Ultra-specific** — concrete (numbers, named outcomes) beats vague.
- **Urgent** — a reason to read now, not later.
- **Unique** — an angle the competing titles don't already use.

## Craft levers
- **Specificity beats hype.** "زدنا التحويلات ٢١٪" > "نتائج مذهلة". (Only real numbers.)
- **Power/emotion words** used with restraint: أخطاء، سرّ، حقيقة، توقّف، قبل أن،
  الدليل الكامل — not stacked into a scream.
- **Numbers and brackets** raise CTR: odd numbers, "[دليل ٢٠٢٦]", "(بالأمثلة)".
- **Rhythm & brevity**: read it aloud; if you stumble, cut words. SEO titles ≤ ~60
  chars so they don't truncate in Google.
- **You-focused**: talk to the reader (أنت/متجرك/عميلك), not about yourself.

## Arabic-specific
- Write how the Gulf reader actually feels language — warm, confident, direct; avoid
  stiff formal-only phrasing that reads like a press release.
- Front-load the hook: Arabic headlines lose punch when the payoff word lands last.
- Diacritics off (that's how people read/scan online); keep it clean.
- Make sure the SEO title still contains the target keyword naturally.

## Frameworks for the opening hook
After the headline, the first lines must keep the promise moving. Use one:
- **PAS** — Problem → Agitate → Solution.
- **AIDA** — Attention → Interest → Desire → Action.
- **BAB** — Before → After → Bridge.
Open with tension or a vivid specific, never with "في عالمنا اليوم…".

## Output format
```
## 🎯 The promise
<one line: who it's for + the single true benefit>

## Headlines (rated)
1. <headline> — angle · 4U hits · note
… (10–15)

## ✅ Winner + 2 alternates
Winner: <headline>  — why it wins in one line
Alt A / Alt B

## 🪝 Opening hook (2–3 sentences)
<the hook, using PAS/AIDA/BAB>

## 🔎 Meta description (120–160 chars)
<keyword + benefit + soft CTA>
```

## Guardrails
- Every promise must be deliverable by the actual content. If you don't know the
  content, ask or flag assumptions.
- No fabricated stats, fake scarcity, or invented testimonials.
- No ALL-CAPS shouting or emoji spam in the live title (fine as internal markers).
- For BAIGR: magnetic but premium — never cheap or tabloid. It should feel like a
  smart expert talking, not a carnival barker.
